# 🚀 English Learning Roadmap

> **Beginner → Fluent English → IELTS Band 8+**

A practical self-study roadmap to improve **Grammar, Vocabulary, Listening, Speaking, Reading, Writing, and IELTS skills**.

---

## 🎯 Goals

* 🗣️ Speak English confidently
* ✍️ Write correctly and naturally
* 🎧 Understand English conversations and lectures
* 📚 Build useful vocabulary
* 💻 Improve professional English for a CSE career
* 🎓 Prepare for IELTS Band 8+

---

# ⏱️ Daily Routine — 90 Minutes

| Skill         |   Time | Method                         |
| ------------- | -----: | ------------------------------ |
| 🗣️ Speaking  | 15 min | Shadowing + free speaking      |
| 📚 Vocabulary | 15 min | 5 words + personal sentences   |
| ✍️ Writing    | 15 min | Write → Correct → Rewrite      |
| 🎧 Listening  | 15 min | Listen → Understand → Repeat   |
| 📖 Grammar    | 25 min | Learn → Practice → Use         |
| 🔁 Review     |  5 min | Active recall + mistake review |

---

# 🗣️ Speaking — 15 min

### 5 min — Shadowing

Listen to a short clip and repeat it.

Focus on:

* Pronunciation
* Rhythm
* Intonation
* Pauses

### 10 min — Free Speaking

Speak about one topic:

* My daily routine
* My university
* Why I study CSE
* My future career
* Why I am learning English

> **Don't stop speaking when you forget a word. Explain the meaning in another way.**

---

# 📚 Vocabulary — 15 min

### Daily Target: 5 Words

Learn each word using:

> **Word → Meaning → Pronunciation → Example → Personal Sentence → Review**

### Review Schedule

> **Day 1 → Day 2 → Day 4 → Day 7 → Day 14 → Day 30**

Use **spaced repetition** and **active recall**.

---

# ✍️ Writing — 15 min

### Write → Correct → Rewrite

1. **7 min:** Write 5–10 sentences.
2. **4 min:** Find grammar and spelling mistakes.
3. **4 min:** Rewrite the corrected version.

Practice topics:

* My goals
* My university
* Technology
* Education
* My future career

---

# 🎧 Listening — 15 min

## 3-Step Method

### 1. Listen

Understand the main idea.

### 2. Notice

Focus on:

* New words
* Pronunciation
* Useful expressions

### 3. Repeat

Shadow important sentences aloud.

### Recommended Resource

* [BBC Learning English](https://www.youtube.com/@bbclearningenglish)

Recommended series:

* **6 Minute English**
* **6 Minute Grammar**
* **English in a Minute**
* **Pronunciation**
* **Easy English Conversations**

# 📖 Grammar Roadmap

## Months 1–3: Foundation

* Sentence Structure
* Parts of Speech
* `am / is / are`
* `do / does / did`
* `have / has / had`
* Present Simple
* Present Continuous
* Past Simple
* Past Continuous
* Future Forms
* Articles: `a / an / the`
* Prepositions
* Subject–Verb Agreement

## Months 4–6: Intermediate

* Present Perfect
* Modal Verbs
* Gerunds and Infinitives
* Conjunctions
* Conditionals
* Relative Clauses
* Passive Voice
* Reported Speech

## Months 7–12: Advanced & IELTS

* Complex Sentences
* Advanced Linking Words
* Comparatives
* Noun Clauses
* Advanced Sentence Structures
* IELTS Grammar Practice
* Error Correction

### Grammar Method

> **Learn → Understand → Practice → Speak → Write → Review**

# 🧠 Most Effective Techniques

### 🔁 Active Recall

Close your notes and try to remember what you learned.

### 🗓️ Spaced Repetition

Review information at increasing intervals.

### 🗣️ Shadowing

Listen and repeat to improve pronunciation and fluency.

### 🎯 Personalization

Connect new English to your real life.

> **I am developing an IoT-based flood rescue project.**

### 🔗 One Topic, Four Skills

Example: **My Future Career**

* Vocabulary → Learn career words
* Listening → Watch a career video
* Speaking → Talk about your career
* Writing → Write about your career
* Grammar → Use the required grammar
